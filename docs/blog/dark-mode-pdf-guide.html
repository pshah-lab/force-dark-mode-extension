How to View and Read PDFs in Dark Mode on Chrome

By Document Accessibility Specialists • Updated September 2026 • 5 min read

Nothing ruins a late-night reading streak like opening an academic paper, textbook, or contract only to be blinded by full-screen white PDF pages. Here is how to achieve true, eye-friendly dark mode for PDFs.

The Problem: Why Chrome's PDF Viewer Ignores Dark Mode

When you open a .pdf file in Google Chrome, it loads through an internal native PDF viewer component (PDFium).

Even if you have dark themes enabled on your computer or browser, the native viewer treats PDF pages as fixed white paper documents. Furthermore, standard content scripts in most browser extensions cannot inject CSS into native PDF plugin tabs, leaving readers stranded.

Common Workarounds (And Why They Fail)

  • OS-Level Screen Invert: Turns the entire screen into inverted colors, which makes your entire desktop looks distorted and inverts colorful diagrams into neon ghosts.
  • Third-Party Cloud Converters: Uploading your PDFs to an external online converter poses critical security and privacy risks, especially for confidential financial, legal, or proprietary work documents.

The Solution: Local Canvas Rendering with ThemeSwitcher

ThemeSwitcher solves this natively by integrating an offline, client-side Dark Document Viewer directly into the extension.

How to Read Online PDFs in Dark Mode:

  1. Open any web PDF link in a Chrome tab.
  2. Click the ThemeSwitcher icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Click the button that appears: Open PDF in dark viewer.
  4. The PDF immediately loads in ThemeSwitcher’s dedicated reader with dark canvas styling applied.

How to Read Local Computer PDFs in Dark Mode:

  1. Click the ThemeSwitcher icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Click Open file viewer at the bottom of the popup.
  3. Click Choose File and select any local PDF, Markdown, or text file on your drive.
  4. Your document renders instantly in soothing dark mode without uploading a single byte to the internet.

Why Saturation-Aware Pixel Conversion Matters

Unlike crude inversion that turns red, blue, and green chart bars into garish neons, ThemeSwitcher's canvas engine samples pixel saturation. Pixels with low saturation (the white paper background) are darkened into deep slate, while colorful lines and illustrations remain vibrant and accurate.

Key Advantages of the ThemeSwitcher PDF Reader:

  • Zero Data Uploads: Built with bundled PDF.js; works entirely offline.
  • Custom Controls: Adjust font sizing, page zoom, and contrast levels.
  • Multi-Format: In addition to PDFs, opens local Markdown (.md), Plain Text (.txt), and RTF files.
  • Multiple Modes: Switch between Smart Dark, Invert, Sepia, and Original views with a single click.
