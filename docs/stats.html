Usage Statistics

Anonymous, combined figures about how ThemeSwitcher is used. No individual install, site or document is ever shown.

These numbers count only people who opted in to anonymous usage statistics, so they understate real use. Groups of fewer than 10 installs are never shown.

What is counted: each opted-in install sends a small anonymous report (a random ID, the UTC day, version numbers, OS family, language and yes/no the theme in use and yes/no flags for engines and features). Nothing about sites, URLs or content is sent. See the privacy policy for the full list and how to delete your data.

All figures cover the last 30 days. This page updates about once an hour.

A retention figure of 0% or 100% means none or all of a small group returned.
