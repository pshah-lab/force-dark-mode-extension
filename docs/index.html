Auto · CSS Engine
Custom Background:

Built to darken pages without breaking them

Simple color inverters turn photos into negatives and flash white on every load. ThemeSwitcher recolors the page the way its designer would have.

It knows when a site is already dark

On load, the Auto engine samples the page and picks the right approach for articles, apps and media-heavy layouts. If a site has its own dark theme, like YouTube or GitHub, it stays untouched.

Dark PDFs, offline

Open local files and web PDFs in a reader built on bundled PDF.js. Paper margins go dark while charts keep their colors. How the dark PDF reader works.

Your colors, per site

Choose pure black (#000000) for OLED screens or a soft slate (#0f1115). Eight built-in themes, a theme editor and per-site settings that sync through your Google account.

No white flash

The dark background is applied at document_start, before the first paint, so new tabs never flash white.

Private by default

No third-party analytics and no tracking pixels. Nothing leaves your device unless you opt in to the usage report or confirm a problem report.

PDFs and documents go dark too

Open a PDF in the built-in reader and the white paper turns dark while charts and images keep their colors. It runs offline, on your device.

  • Web PDFs open in the dark reader from the popup
  • Local files open without uploading anywhere
  • Google Docs pages stay light, because Google draws them on a canvas. The toolbars and menus do darken

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ThemeSwitcher, privacy, and how it works.

A dark mode extension dynamically modifies webpage CSS to replace blinding white backgrounds with eye-friendly dark slate or pitch black tones. It ensures high text contrast while preserving photographs, illustrations, and videos.
Add ThemeSwitcher from the Chrome Web Store. Once installed, click the extension icon in your browser toolbar on any website to toggle dark mode on or off. The extension will remember your choice for that specific domain.
Yes. The Auto Engine adapts to articles, wikis, social networks, and single-page apps. If a site already features an active dark theme (such as YouTube in dark mode), ThemeSwitcher detects this and leaves the site’s native styling alone.
Yes, ThemeSwitcher is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. It processes pages and documents on your device with no third-party analytics or tracking. Nothing is sent unless you opt in (the usage report in versions 2.0.0 and 2.1.0) or confirm a broken-site report (version 2.1.0 and later); your settings and documents never leave your device.
When you open a PDF or click "Open PDF in dark viewer" in the popup, ThemeSwitcher opens the document in an offline reader powered by local PDF.js. It converts pixels using a saturation-aware algorithm that preserves colors in charts and illustrations while darkening white page backgrounds.
Not on the document itself. Google Docs draws the page on a canvas, and no CSS-based dark mode can recolor canvas content. The toolbars and menus around it do darken.
It is built for Chrome, and Chromium-based browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store can use it too. In Microsoft Edge, turn on “Allow extensions from other stores” first.
