Dark Mode (Computing)

ThemeSwitcher Dark Mode Active — Clean contrast, preserved imagery, and relaxed reading.

Layout-Safe Typography

Text contrast is automatically calibrated to meet WCAG AA standards without breaking font hierarchies or margins.

Preserved Media & Photos

Unlike crude color inverters, photographs, videos, and canvas elements remain completely natural and un-inverted.

Per-Site Memory

Settings are stored locally in Chrome Sync per domain, ensuring your preferences persist across visits and devices.

Engineered for Quality, Speed, and Eye Comfort

Most dark mode extensions either bruteforce color inversion or inject thousands of slow CSS rules. ThemeSwitcher takes an algorithmic, performance-first approach.

Intelligent Auto Engine

Scans page luminance, DOM structure, and media density to apply the optimal dark strategy. If a site natively supports dark mode (like YouTube or GitHub), it leaves it untouched.

Local PDF & Document Dark Reader

Say goodbye to blinding white PDF tabs. The built-in document viewer uses bundled PDF.js with saturation-aware canvas pixel conversion so diagrams and text stay readable offline.

CSS Override Engine

Recolors backgrounds, typography, forms, inputs, and borders using mathematical contrast palettes. Smart detection selectively inverts black logos on dark backgrounds.

Custom Background Palettes

Prefer deep pitch black (#000000) for OLED displays or a softer midnight slate (#0f1115) for evening reading? Choose any custom background hex color per domain right from the popup.

Zero White Flashes

Injected at document_start with CSS custom property bindings before the DOM renders. Your eyes are never subjected to blinding flashes between page navigations.

100% Offline & Zero Tracking

No telemetry. No tracking pixels. No analytics beacons. No external servers. Your browsing history never leaves your device, and preferences sync only via your personal Google account.
